## The product
**Disc Golf Labs Putting Course** — a 5-day putting course (one focused lesson + one drill per day) paired with the DGL putting app, which tracks make % by distance and builds a daily-practice habit. This is what the marketing pages sell.

## The mission / why
> "Most players don't need more practice. They need practice with real intent and direction."

Disc Golf Labs is a passion project combining disc golf, teaching, and technology. It exists to break players out of **tutorial hell** — the cycle of watching videos without ever internalizing or applying the lessons.

## The methodology
**Reinforcement Learning + Micro Learning:**
- Learn in small doses.
- Practice immediately.
- Get statistical feedback.
- Practice with intent beats more practice without it.
- "Putting is a muscle" — work it out with the right intent, not just volume.

## The 5-day arc
- **Day 1 — Foundations.** Methodology, the 3 putting styles (spin / push / spin-push), the 3 foundations every putt shares (**Lever, Spin, Power**), and the putting app. Drill: first tracked round.
- **Day 2 — Consistent stroke.** Spin from the fingers + the lever (arm). Release. Drills: *Self Toss*, *Build the Release @ 10–15 ft*.
- **Day 3 — Adding power.** Stagger & straddle putts, weight transfer, the legs drive power.
- **Day 4 — Pre-shot routine.** Build & drill a routine; the *Place & Putt* drill. Solves "I make every putt in the backyard, then miss in tournaments."
- **Day 5 — Taking it to the course.** Flattening your putt, the *Pole Drill*, keep practicing.

## Signature vocabulary (reuse in copy)
- **Loaded position** — the moment your routine reaches the point where the only movement left is forward.
- **Rule of 2** — pick 2 mental cues per session; drill them into every putt.
- **Aim small, miss small** — choose a single chain link; release at exactly that point.
- **"Give the basket a handshake"** — leave your arm extended after release; self-diagnose the finish.
- **Fewer variables = more consistency** — every hinge/lever is a variable; reduce them.
- **Putting is a muscle** — reps with intent grow it; reps without plateau it.
