## Intro

- The power in your putt comes from adding your lower body into the putt. Not all from your arm. Moving your arm faster may add power, but you lose consistency. Use your body and your larger muscles to do the heavy lifting. Leaving your arm and hand to focus on the release and consistency.

---

### Stagger Putts

- Stagger putts are the more traditional path to your main putting style, but you should choose whichever suites your game best.
- Stagger putts get their name sake from your front foot being out infront of yourself, and your back leg being staggered behind it. Staggering your body towards the target.
- The power in your stagger putt comes from your back leg and timing of weight transfer.
- We start with your lever entering the loaded position. At that point, when your arm is in it’s loaded position and you have all your weight on your back leg. You then kick with your back leg and move forward your arm in sync. This creates a load balance. Your back leg will kick and move your momentum forward giving you velocity on your putt. Your arm moving in unison will allow you to keep balance and add power.
- Practice this by starting in your loaded position and exploding your weight forward. This will give you a feel for timing and how to control the power.
- Tip for kicking your leg. I personally like to think that I am kicking myself in the butt. When I do it helps get my leg higher. This helps me release my putt higher. I notice I miss low more when my leg just kicks out and not up.

---

### Straddle Putts

- Straddle Putts are needed for almost all putting styles when a tree or obstacle are in your putting stance.
- You start with your shoulders square with the basket, feet should be shoulders length apart. You’ll notice your arm has lots of room to move. Try to get some weight transfer. Feel how it differs from the straddle. You should feel your legs generate most of the power making it great for jump putting.
- When you are in the loaded position you will find your arm is loaded like before, but your weight will be on the balls of your feet. Your legs will have a slight squat to them.
- Practice the weight transfer by starting from the loaded position. Feel how your hips hinge and your knees bend some. You are making yourself a spring. You are creating tension getting to the loaded position. This tension is then released by driving your weight through your feet, while activating your lever to sync it with your weight transfer, and then releasing the disc from your fingers as the last motion in the putt.
- A fun trick with your straddle putt to add power is the cherry picker. If you ever did this stretch as a kid you know that you stand in a straddle and you reach your hands behind your legs as far as you can go. We can do that same thing with our reach back. Get to the loaded position, then reach further back like in the cherry picker. Now with your further reach back try syncing your legs and arm again. Did you feel more power or spin? I personally use this trick to get more distance on my jump putts. It creates more leverage by creating a longer lever for you to gain power with. Throw a few putts like this and see how much more power it provides.

---

### Summary

All in all, your putting stance preference is entirely up to you and your style. Professionals use both of these styles during rounds, and it is well worth having both of them well practiced. Today’s putting game I want you to do one round with the stagger, and one round with straddle. Get a feel for both, you may like the other more than you expected. Tomorrow we will delve into routines in putting, and how to effectively use them in your game.
