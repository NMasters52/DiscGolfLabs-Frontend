And I've noticed the same problem over and over again.

Most players don't need more practice. They need practice with real intent and direction.

That's why I'm excited to announce the Disc Golf Labs Putting Course, launching on [DATE].

This course is designed to help you understand your putting form, break down putting to the basics, and learn how to make meaningful improvements on your own. More importantly, you'll learn how to take what you do in practice and transfer it to the course when the putt actually matters.

Disc Golf Labs is a passion project that combines my love for disc golf, teaching, and technology. Everything in this course is built around the lessons I've learned through years of competition, coaching, and countless hours on the practice green.

The course is delivered in five simple lessons spread across 5 days. Each lesson includes focused instruction and a practical drill designed to reinforce what you've learned. Through the Disc Golf Labs platform, you'll be able to track your progress, measure your results, and identify areas for improvement over time.

My goal is simple: give you a clear roadmap to becoming a more confident and consistent putter.

I'm incredibly proud of what we've built, and I can't wait to share it with the disc golf community.
